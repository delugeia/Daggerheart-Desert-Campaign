## A–D

- **A Life Remembered** — Prologue in which the new characters awaken with emotional echoes of the previous world. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Akh** — A transformed or effective spirit; Nekhar is described as an Exiled Akh. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Altenis** — Occasional spelling variant of Altennis in image-prompt history. See **Altennis**.
- **Altennis** — Zeb’s elf Rogue/Nightwalker, former spy, and partial copier of the ’Iiskir Elixir recipe. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Amarna** — Largest walled city in the river valley and site of the great temple of Ra. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Amun** — Ancient creator believed by heretics to be imprisoned beneath the Bowels of Anubis. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Amun-Ra** — Dominant public god, commonly called Ra; official myth treats him as the merged result of Ra consuming Amun. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Anubis** — God of proper burial, passage, judgment, and the boundary between life and death. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **The Artery** — Secret underground river flowing through a hidden world beneath the desert. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **Ashen Vow** — Experienced adventuring party mysteriously killed inside the ruined city. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Ash-Salt Dead** — Undead encountered in the Hall of Thirsting Dead. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **Ashur-Ka** — Fire djinn freed by the party from an enchanted red vessel. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Ba** — The mobile, individual personality or identity of a soul. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Bogsnard the Blue / Bogs** — Noah’s previous-life gnome abjuration wizard. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Bowels of Anubis** — Bottomless healing and agonizing oasis used for punishment and believed to imprison Amun. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Bound** — Early remade-world category for oppressed or punished people. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Burning Jewel** — Fiery key used to open the buried catacomb stair and associated with the Cinderheart Seal. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Captain Jenna Marshall** — Principal captain and voice of the miniature community within Intrepid. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Catacombs of Ashur-Ka** — Buried temple complex containing the Hall of Thirsting Dead, Scorpion Well, Cinderheart sanctum, and djinn prison. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Cave of Shattered Reflection** — Site of the old heroes’ final battle against Vecna. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Cinderheart Seal** — Ritual seal recovered from the catacombs and entrusted to Neshet. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Cinderquartz** — Newly appearing magical quartz produced by the mine in The Quartz Below. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Corpsebearer Shade** — Solemn undead bearer that returned the Ashen Vow’s bodies. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **Cult of the Withered Hand** — Cult that abducted the people later miniaturized and hidden within Intrepid. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Duneveil Stalker** — Magical desert feline predator encountered near the ruined city. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).

## E–K

- **The Empty Sea** — Common translation or epithet for the Sharian desert. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **Ernie K. the Fourth** — Andrew’s previous-life elf champion fighter. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Favored** — Early remade-world category for privileged but watched inhabitants. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Flame Guardian** — Tier 2 boss-like guardian developed for a fire or temple encounter. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **Forgotten Desert City** — Lost city believed to contain scrolls unseen for millennia. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Garrick** — Maul-wielding member of the Ashen Vow. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Glassling Serpent / Glass Snake** — Glass-bodied serpent associated with the Scorpion Well. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **Great Temple of Ra** — Central temple in Amarna and seat of the dominant priesthood. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Hall of Thirsting Dead** — Central catacomb chamber containing Ash-Salt Dead and an eastern statue puzzle. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Hound Master** — Higher-tier adversary designed to command supernatural hounds. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **Intrepid** — Jason’s Guardian/Stalwart; a rebuilt Clank body inhabited by miniaturized abductees. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **’Iiskir Elixir** — Restricted legendary formula partially copied by Altennis. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Isfet** — Disorder and corrupt unmaking opposed to Ma’at. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Jengo Dustpaw** — Noah’s current player character and sixth member of the party. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Ka** — Sustaining life-force or spiritual double. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Kaelen the Unyielding** — Dwarf guardian and member of the Ashen Vow. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Kas** — Ancient anomaly who appears as a finely dressed bard-like figure and remembers the previous reality. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Khat’Najat** — “The Lifeline,” the great river sustaining the settled valley. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **Khepri** — Neshet’s assistant or agent in the Stillwater Covenant. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **King Sabet** — Dead king represented through four portrait identities or life stages in a tomb riddle. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).

## L–R

- **The Last Secret** — Epilogue describing Vecna’s victory and the Unraveling. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Lost Temple of Anubis** — Planned investigation site where previous visitors vanished and undead violate sacred order. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Ma’at** — Goddess and principle of truth, justice, balance, and right order; Sylvester’s patron. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Merik the Unwanted** — Justin’s previous-life aasimar celestial warlock. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Mulch / Fungi** — Justin’s current Fungril druid, symbiotic survivor, and dream-bearer. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Nabira** — Desert witch associated with King Sabet’s tomb and accompanied by two hounds. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Najm Plant** — Valley plant whose irregularly blooming flower is required for the ’Iiskir Elixir. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Neith** — Goddess of weaving, creation, endurance, and unity; patron of Intrepid. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Nekhar, the First Lich** — Campaign demigod, Exiled Akh, crow figure, and rebel against Ma’at. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Neshet** — Local leader of the Stillwater Covenant and recipient of the Cinderheart Seal. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Neverwinter** — Old-world city above which the fallen heroes witnessed the Unraveling. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Nullium** — Anti-magic ore found in the Pharaoh’s Teeth. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Oil of Anubis** — Ritual oil needed for safe descent into the Bowels of Anubis. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **The Pharaoh** — Unnamed ruler who claims to be Amun’s reincarnation and opposes Ra’s priesthood. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **The Pharaoh’s Teeth** — Mineral-rich western mountain range containing the penal mines. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **Priesthood of Anubis** — Religious institution expected to help create the Oil of Anubis. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Priesthood of Ra** — Dominant religious institution, recently removed from the Pharaoh’s authority. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Qurfa Tree** — Rare valley tree whose bark is required for the ’Iiskir Elixir. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).
- **Ra** — Dominant sun god and public ruler of divine order; common name for Amun-Ra. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Renenutet** — Goddess of nourishment, harvest, agriculture, sustenance, stewardship, family continuity, and pragmatic survival; patron of Mulch “Fungi.” See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Rod of Seven Parts** — Completed old-world artifact used in the final attempt to stop Vecna. See [Artifacts and Important Objects](ArtifactsAndImportantObjects.md).

## S–Z

- **Sahrem, the Emberbound Warden** — Named guardian of the Inner Sanctum of the Cinderheart. See [NPCs](NPCs.md).
- **Scorpion Well** — Catacomb chamber with a dry well, side rooms, cave-in, Tomb Scorpion, and Glassling Serpent. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Sedric Runeborne** — Rob’s Aetheris Bard/Wordsmith and knowledge-seeker. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Sharian** — Vast desert beyond the Pharaoh’s Teeth, also called the Empty Sea. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **Stillwater Covenant** — Hidden Amun-aligned organization led locally by Neshet. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Sun Dogs** — Ra’s secret agents, investigators, and religious enforcers. See [Factions and Organizations](FactionsAndOrganizations.md).
- **Sylcaryn Cadraldrivi / Syl** — Jason’s previous-life elf trickery cleric and final surviving hero of the Vecna battle. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Sylvester Dalone** — AJ’s current faun war wizard and framed detective. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Tallhut** — AJ’s previous-life goliath ranger/scout. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Temeir-Al / Temeir** — Zeb’s previous-life elf shadow monk. See [Player Characters](PlayerCharacters.md).
- **Thoth** — God of knowledge, writing, and scholarship; Sedric’s patron. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Tomb Scorpion** — Large scorpion adversary in the Scorpion Well. See [Creatures and Adversaries](CreaturesAndAdversaries.md).
- **The Treacherous Land** — Especially dangerous region within the Sharian. See [Geography](Geography.md).
- **The Quartz Below** — Extended mine crawl centered on Cinderquartz and a missing investigator. See [Adventures and Events](AdventuresAndEvents.md).
- **The Swallowed Stair** — Buried stair opened by the Burning Jewel and leading into the catacombs. See [Locations](Locations.md).
- **Unwritten** — Early remade-world category for the majority living ordinary lives. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Vecna** — Lich-god who won the final battle and remade the multiverse. See [History and Cosmology](HistoryAndCosmology.md).
- **Wepwawet** — Opener of the Ways and patron of Altennis. See [Religion and Gods](ReligionAndGods.md).
- **Well of Anubis** — Source named in the ’Iiskir recipe; relationship to the Bowels is unresolved. See [Locations](Locations.md).
