## Notable Style Revision

Early transition images used black-and-white, rough 16:9 storyboards. Later campaign art established a different default:

- Full color.
- Cinematic fantasy RPG sourcebook illustration.
- Painterly realism.
- Believable anatomy, materials, lighting, and scale.
- Ancient, weathered desert-world texture.
- Serious and ominous rather than exaggerated.
- Restrained, diegetic magic.

Storyboard style remains valid only when explicitly requested. It does not override the current default.
