Daggerheart Desert Campaign Encyclopedia

Version: 1.0
Canon through: September 17, 2026

This file is the entry point for the encyclopedia. All files use a flat directory structure and simple relative links.

Canon Conventions

  • All project material is treated as canon unless a later conversation or document overrides it.
  • Later decisions take precedence over earlier drafts.
  • Meaningful revisions are identified in GM Notes.
  • Rumors, religious claims, incomplete research, and unresolved theories remain framed as such.
  • Official Daggerheart books are referenced where useful, but their general rules and setting text are not reproduced.
  • Player names appear only to identify who plays a character. Personal player details are excluded.

Major Notable Revisions

  • Intrepid’s origin: The early “tiny cadets stole a Clank” concept was replaced. Intrepid is now the rebuilt husk of a once-sentient Clank, inhabited by magically miniaturized abductees led by Captain Jenna Marshall.
  • Intrepid’s old artifact hook: The earliest idea that one shared society depended on a failing artifact no longer fits the revised origin and remains unresolved rather than current fact.
  • Public cosmology: Early remade-world planning described an openly Vecna-centered society. Later campaign material presents Ra as the dominant public deity. Vecna’s metaphysical authorship remains canon, but the relationship between the two structures is unresolved.
  • Visual style: Early epilogue work used black-and-white storyboard art. The later art-style.html establishes full-color cinematic fantasy RPG sourcebook art as the default; storyboard style is now used only when explicitly requested.
  • Current roster (September 17, 2026): The current party contains six characters, including Nakhtresh “Nakht,” Justin’s new character after Mulch’s death in the last quest. Mulch’s entry remains as a historical record. The previous-life party remains historical material rather than a mechanical template.
