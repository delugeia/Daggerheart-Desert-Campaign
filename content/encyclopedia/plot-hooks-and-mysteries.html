Main Campaign Mysteries

Amun, Ra, and the Bowels

  • Is Amun truly imprisoned beneath the Bowels of Anubis?
  • Did Ra defeat him, imprison him, consume him, or do something more complicated?
  • Are prisoners sustaining Amun, weakening him, feeding the prison, or serving another purpose?
  • Is the whispering voice truly Amun?
  • Why did it tell the Pharaoh to claim reincarnation, if that account is true?
  • What will happen if the party reaches, contacts, or frees Amun?
  • Is Amun trustworthy merely because Ra may be lying?

Ra and Vecna

  • How does Ra exist within a reality remade by Vecna?
  • Is Ra an independent god, a rewritten being, Vecna’s mask, Vecna’s agent, or a rival anomaly?
  • Why does public history center Ra rather than Vecna?
  • What can Vecna perceive or control now?
  • What does Ra know about the previous reality and the party’s echoes?

The Nature of Godhood

  • Are the recognized gods truly gods?
  • What distinguishes a god from a sufficiently powerful immortal, alien, or reality-shaper?
  • Do the anti-god skeptics possess evidence?
  • How do Nekhar, djinn, vampires, and Vecna complicate ordinary theology?

Character Mysteries

Altennis

  • Who hired him to copy the ’Iiskir scroll?
  • Who arranged the capture and what was the real objective?
  • What is the missing ingredient and complete brewing process?
  • What does the elixir actually do?
  • What claims does the former spy guild retain over him?

Sedric Runeborne

  • Who sent his vision?
  • Why were those specific mine prisoners marked by destiny?
  • What is in the forgotten desert library?
  • Is his pursuit of knowledge drawing him toward pre-remaking history?

Intrepid

  • Why did the Cult of the Withered Hand abduct these particular people?
  • What atrocity did the cult commission?
  • Where is the mage who saved them?
  • Why do unrelated inhabitants share dreams of being torn apart?
  • Did the cult identify people with past-life memories?
  • What happened to the original Clank who owned the body?
  • Can the early failing-artifact idea be revised into a problem affecting the vessel itself?

Sylvester Dalone

  • Why did his detective agency frame him?
  • What case had he reached?
  • Who inside the agency is responsible?
  • Is it connected to Ra, the Sun Dogs, a cartel, or the Stillwater Covenant?
  • Who becomes his recurring arch-enemy?

Mulch “Fungi”

Historical unresolved hooks: Mulch died in the last quest. These questions remain unresolved; his death does not establish answers or automatically transfer these hooks to Nakhtresh.

  • Who is hunting him and why?
  • Is the symbiosis benign, necessary, manipulative, or all three?
  • What are the ancient fungi?
  • Why does Mulch experience other people’s nightmares?
  • Do those nightmares connect to the Unraveling?

Jengo Dustpaw

Jengo’s full current source-derived details are collected in PlayerCharacters.html. Any unresolved backstory, patron, ancestry, or personal objective recorded there should be incorporated into the main plot as later information is confirmed.

Faction and Political Mysteries

Sun Dogs

  • How do they identify and track the party?
  • Who commands them?
  • Are they loyal to Ra personally, the priesthood, or their own doctrine?
  • Have they infiltrated Sylvester’s agency or Altennis’s guild?

Stillwater Covenant

  • How much does the Covenant actually know about Amun?
  • Was Khepri’s staged ambush a test, deception, or protection?
  • What does Neshet intend to do with the Cinderheart Seal?
  • Can the Covenant be trusted when its methods rely on secrecy and manipulation?

Cult of the Withered Hand

  • What is its doctrine and leadership?
  • Is the “withered hand” connected to Vecna, necromancy, decay, or a different symbol?
  • What did it need from Intrepid’s inhabitants?
  • Does it know about the old multiverse?

Pharaoh and Priesthood

  • Which institutions remain loyal to the Pharaoh after Ra’s proclamation?
  • Does the Pharaoh sincerely hear Amun?
  • Is his reincarnation claim delusion, manipulation, or hidden truth?
  • Will the political split become civil war?

Location and Adventure Mysteries

The Ruined City and the Ashen Vow

  • What killed the Ashen Vow without obvious injury?
  • Why were the bodies returned?
  • Are Corpsebearer Shades servants, witnesses, jailers, or mourners?
  • Why was the junior party told not to enter?
  • Is the city connected to Sedric’s forgotten library?

The Cinderquartz Mine

  • What created the new quartz?
  • Is it reacting to the Cinderheart Seal?
  • What happened to Neshet’s contact?
  • Are shard creatures alive, possessed, or extensions of the mine?
  • What makes resting dangerous?
  • Is Cinderquartz valuable enough to start a political or commercial conflict?

Lost Temple of Anubis

  • Why do investigators vanish?
  • Who or what raised the undead?
  • Is the temple protecting something, imprisoning something, or being corrupted from below?
  • What ingredient or rite for the Oil of Anubis survives there?

The Artery

  • How was its existence learned?
  • What people and creatures live along it?
  • Does it connect to the Bowels, Khat’Najat, or the Cinderquartz mine?
  • Is it a natural river, underworld passage, or divine circulatory system?

King Sabet’s Tomb

  • Which portrait or life-stage represents the truth?
  • Why do the images transform into skeletal forms?
  • What does Nabira want from the tomb?

Reusable Early Hooks

Vanishing Mine Shaft

Workers disappear in a shaft. Search parties also fail to return. This can be linked to Cinderquartz, the Artery, undead, a buried temple, or a separate creature.

Poison the Well

A cartel or cult plans to introduce disease into a water source, possibly as part of a demon-raising ritual. Because water sustains the entire civilization, the plot is both mass murder and political destabilization.

The Sealed Crate

The party is hired to deliver a sealed crate. An apparent roadside ambush distracts them while covert agents exchange the chest. The original concept reveals a child in deathlike stasis, raising questions about vampires, sacrifice, and cult agendas.

Repeating Street Urchin

The same child approaches different adults on different days, asks for help, and leads them away. The child may be bait, illusion, shapeshifter, victim, or repeated memory.

Cursed City Support Mission

A low-level party serves as support for stronger heroes, is ordered not to enter a ruin, and must respond when the experts return dead. This hook developed into the Ashen Vow expedition.

Superseded or Revision-Required Hooks

Intrepid’s Shared-Society Artifact

The original hook depended upon all inhabitants sharing one society with a failing artifact. The revised origin gives them many unrelated backgrounds. Preserve only the possibility of a failing vessel component or a new shared need.

“THING” in the Temple

The placeholder objective was replaced by the developed Cinderheart and Ashur-Ka material.

Open Vecna Worship

Early world design made Vecna’s supremacy overt. Later public religion centers Ra. The metaphysical truth remains, but open Vecna worship should not be assumed without deciding how the two layers connect.
